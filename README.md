# Monthly Sales Performance Overview

An interactive Excel dashboard analyzing a year of sales data across regions, sales channels, product categories, and top-performing sales reps — built as part of my Data Analytics training with the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI).

![Dashboard Preview](Final_Dashboard.png)

## Overview

This dashboard consolidates raw sales transaction data into a single interactive view, letting a viewer slice performance by month, region, sales channel, and product category using built-in slicers.

## Key Metrics

- **Total Sales:** $3.5M
- **Total Orders:** 2,000
- **Top Performer:** Mei Tan ($319.6k)

## Features

- **KPI Cards** — Total Sales, Total Orders, and Top Performer at a glance
- **Revenue by Region** — bar chart comparing Central, North, East, South, and West
- **Revenue by Sales Channel** — donut chart breaking down Corporate Sales, Marketplace, Online, and Retail Store
- **Top 10 Performance** — ranked bar chart of the top 10 sales reps by revenue
- **Revenue by Category / Product** — category and product-level revenue breakdown
- **Revenue per Month** — trend line across the full year, with H1 and H2 peak months highlighted
- **Interactive Slicers** — filter the whole dashboard by month, region, sales channel, and product category

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, Dashboard design, Helper column technique)

## How to Use

1. Download `Final_2nd_Dashboard_Monthly_Sales_Performance_Overview.xlsx`
2. Open it in Excel (macros not required)
3. Use the slicers on the right side of the dashboard to filter by month, region, sales channel, or product category

## Key Insights

- The Central region leads all regions in revenue, with Central and North together accounting for a large share of total sales
- Online and Corporate Sales are the two largest sales channels, together making up over 60% of revenue
- Computers is by far the top-performing product category, outperforming the next closest category by nearly 3x
- The Gaming Laptop is the single highest-revenue product
